YAHTZEE COACH v42.5 — DEPLOYMENT FROM v42 / v41.1

1. Unzip yahtzee_coach_streamlit_game_v42_5.zip.
2. Open the yahtzee_coach_game_v42_5 folder.
3. In GitHub, open the ROOT of TealMichael/yahtzee-coach.
4. Click Add file -> Upload files.
5. Upload these files:
   - app.py
   - exact_mode.py
   - exact_policy.npz
   - puzzle_bank.py
   - puzzle_bank.npz
   - challenge_catalog.npz
   - session_learning.py
   - README.md (recommended)
6. IMPORTANT: exact_policy.npz, puzzle_bank.npz, and challenge_catalog.npz must sit beside app.py.
7. Commit with:
   Expand exact puzzle universe for Daily Challenge - v42.5
8. Wait for Streamlit Community Cloud to redeploy.
9. Open the normal app and play several rounds.
10. Add ?solver=1 to the URL and confirm:
    - Exact count rises
    - Fallbacks stay at 0
11. You should begin seeing a much broader mix of scorecards, including:
    - true endgame (1–2 boxes open)
    - upper bonus already secured
    - upper bonus mathematically gone
    - Joker / extra-Yahtzee contexts
    - both Roll 1 and Roll 2

v42 progress/mastery/mobile features are preserved.
The shared Daily Challenge leaderboard/database is NOT live yet; v42.5 prepares the puzzle universe for v43.
